**PriceTag** — monospace tabular-nums ¥ price, the loudest mono voice on the mirror.

```jsx
<PriceTag amount={40700} size="lg" countUp />
<PriceTag amount={8900} size="md" />
```

Sizes: `sm` 16 · `md` 24 · `lg` 40 · `xl` 56. `countUp` eases to the value on mount (used on the confirm total; snaps under reduced-motion). `tone="accent"` tints cobalt.
